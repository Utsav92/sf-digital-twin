This is the San Francisco digital twin, built in Unreal Engine five point eight. Real terrain from USGS three D E P, streets and buildings from Open Street Map and Data S F, projected into UTM zone ten and cut into six hundred eighteen tiles.
Every building is Nanite geometry. A detail layer adds cornices, balconies, window sills and railings, about ninety million extra triangles, and two hundred eight thousand trees are placed from the survey data.
Lumen provides dynamic global illumination and reflections, so light bounces between the towers, and Virtual Shadow Maps keep shadows crisp from skyscraper to curb.
At street level, curbs, lane markings and crosswalks come straight from the road data, and every tile matches its source geometry to one centimetre.
World Partition streams the whole city, and from the bay you see it all in one scene, with Temporal Super Resolution reconstructing a clean image.
Cinematic cameras use real film back, focal length and aperture, with photo mode and look presets for the final grade.
